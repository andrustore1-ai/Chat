Open:
https://YOUR-DOMAIN/webhook-admin.html

1) Enter POLL_SECRET.
2) Press "تفعيل Webhook" once.
3) IMPORTANT: GREEN API documentation says setSettings reboots the instance and settings may take up to 5 minutes to apply.
4) After the instance returns authorized, send a NEW WhatsApp message.
5) Press "فحص الحالة".

Expected:
- green.webhookExact = true
- green.incomingEnabled = true
- green.stateInstance = authorized
- webhookHealth.lastReceived appears after GREEN API sends a POST
- webhookHealth.lastSuccess appears after the AI reply is sent

Do not use /api/http-mode or /api/poll. They are disabled in this package.
