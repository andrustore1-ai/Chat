Built-in behavior:

- Duplicate-response guard prevents sending the same answer verbatim again.
- Short followups such as بس؟ / طيب؟ / كيف؟ are treated using previous conversation context.
- Manager rules are passed to the AI as mandatory behavior/style rules, without allowing invention of facts.
